WHY THIS REUNION?

Ye Reunion Kyun?

School khatam hue 4 saal ho gaye hain...

In 4 saalon mein hum sab apni-apni life mein aage badh gaye. Kisi ne college join kiya, kisi ne job, kisi ne business... kuch naye log mile, kuch naye shehar mile, aur life dheere-dheere badalti gayi.

Lekin ek cheez wahi reh gayi...

Hamari school wali yaadein. ❤️

Wahi classroom, woh last bench, teachers ki daant, bina wajah ki hasi, lunch ke time ki bakchodi, exams ka pressure aur sabse important — woh dost jo school life ko special banate the.

Pichli baar hum mile the... aur shayad tab humein pata nahi tha ki agli mulaqat ke liye phir itna waqt lag jayega.

Isliye is baar socha...

Ek aur baar sabko ek jagah bulaya jaye. 🫂

Na kisi ka status matter karega, na koi kahan pahunch gaya hai...

Bas ek shaam ke liye hum phir se wahi honge — school ke woh students, jo kabhi ek saath haste the. ❤️

17 October sirf ek date nahi hai.

It's a chance to pause life for one evening... and make one more memory together.

Ho sakta hai kal hum sab phir apni-apni duniya mein busy ho jayein...

Isliye...
is baar mil lete hain. 🫂
